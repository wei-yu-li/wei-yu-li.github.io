About Me

I am a Ph.D. student in Applied Mathematics at Harvard University. My research interests lie in high-dimensional statistics and machine learning theory, random matrix theory, and distributed optimization. I am particularly interested in developing precise mathematical characterizations of modern learning and inference problems.

Before Harvard, I received my B.Sc. in Mathematics and Applied Mathematics from the School of the Gifted Young at University of Science and Technology of China (USTC).
