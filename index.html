About

I am a Ph.D. student in Applied Mathematics at Harvard University. My research interests lie in high-dimensional statistics and machine learning theory, random matrix theory, and distributed optimization. I am particularly interested in developing precise mathematical characterizations of modern learning and inference problems.

Before Harvard, I received my B.Sc. in Mathematics and Applied Mathematics from the School of the Gifted Young at the University of Science and Technology of China.

Research Areas

  • High-dimensional statistics and machine learning theory: theoretical foundations for learning and inference in complex, high-dimensional models.

  • Random matrix theory: spectral methods and asymptotic tools for analyzing high-dimensional data.

  • Distributed optimization: communication-efficient and robust learning over decentralized systems.
